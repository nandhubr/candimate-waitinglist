Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Candimate helps candidates preparing for public exams find others from their own locality who are travelling to the same exam centre. This policy explains what we collect on our waitlist page at candimate.in, why we collect it and what your choices are.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist you give us:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your upcoming exam (optional)

We do not ask for your address, ID documents or payment details on this page.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to send you the link to the Candimate bot when it launches, and to share launch updates. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to Candimate.

Who handles your data

We use a few service providers to run this page:

  • Supabase stores your waitlist details in a database. Its servers may be outside India.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts supplies the page’s typeface, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

If you arrive from an ad on Instagram or another platform, that platform collects its own data under its own policy. We don’t control that.

Cookies and tracking

This page does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other sites.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until the bot has launched and for as long afterwards as is needed to run Candimate, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop sending you updates. Email us at the address below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. We aim to handle your data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

Your details are stored in a database that the public cannot read, and the page uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take care to protect what you share.

Children

Candimate is meant for adults preparing for public exams. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

The Candimate bot

When the Candimate bot launches on Telegram, it will handle your information differently, such as your exam centre and locality. We will publish a separate notice explaining that before you use it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact
For any privacy question or request, email candimatein@gmail.com.
