Questions

Good to know before you join.

Is my phone number shared?

Your phone number is never shown to other candidates. You choose whether to accept a chat request, and you can block or report anyone.

How are matches made?

You enter your exam centre, exam date and locality. We prioritise candidates from your own locality heading to the same centre on the same day, sorted by distance.

Is Candimate free?

Yes, a free plan is available so you can find and connect with fellow candidates. We’ll tell you clearly if anything ever becomes paid.

What if I don’t like a match?

Block or report them in one tap. Nobody can contact you without your say.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Candimate works inside Telegram, even on a basic phone and slow data.

When does it launch?

Soon. Join the early community and we’ll send you the bot link as soon as it’s ready.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, phone number and email only to send you the bot link and launch updates.
